01 · Modular toolbox

One technological core. Different ways to deploy.

Point at a module — the figure follows

01

Integrations and ingestion

Continuous, incremental sync running entirely on EU infrastructure, with change detection and deletion propagation on every cycle. Rapidly expanding coverage across European knowledge systems — every use case our customers request is within reach.

02

Knowledge base

Knowledge Graph hosted in the EU or own cloud, with full export and built-in knowledge protection incl. permissions enforced at the individual fact. Compatible by MCP with any AI Assistant, making outcomes relevant and reliable, without compromising knowledge.

03

Search and agents

Knowledge Graph paired with its own AI Assistant (can be white-labeled), reachable through a web interface or as a chatbot inside Slack or Microsoft Teams. One admin panel to govern model, data, and tool usage across the company. Full support of common models, incl. EU-hosted Anthropic models.
