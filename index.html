Knowledge Base

What makes it trustworthy.

  • Universal integration. Connects to every system that holds company knowledge — including tools like AFAS and DATEV that have no off-the-shelf MCP.
  • Full control, current and compounding. You own everything — the knowledge you start with, and everything the AI compounds on top of it — never the vendor.
  • EU data residency. Stays in Europe, on European-owned infrastructure — no US-owned dependency anywhere in the chain.
  • Fact-level permissions. Governs access down to the individual fact, so the AI assistant can act freely without ever exposing what a user shouldn’t see.
  • Sensitive-data masking. Blocks sensitive information such as IBANs from ever reaching the LLM.
  • Continuous maintenance. Actively checks for outdated information and inconsistencies, keeping the source of truth accurate over time.
  • Multi-hop reasoning support. Structures knowledge so complex, inferential questions get real answers — not just simple lookups.
  • Full audit trail. Logs what was retrieved, by whom, and when — protection and accountability built into every answer.
AI Assistant

What teams use.

  • Natural-language interface. Makes the knowledge base accessible through simple conversation — no query language required.
  • Advanced agentic capabilities. Executes tasks and workflows across the knowledge base — agents propose, people approve.
  • Web access for external information. Reaches beyond company knowledge for public or external context — no need to switch tools to search elsewhere.
  • Persistent context and memory. Carries context forward across a conversation and related chats within a project, so answers stay coherent without repeating yourself.
  • Meets you where you work. Available through the web, Slack, and Microsoft Teams — no context-switching to a separate tool.
