A slash, a linden, and back again.

The name before the name

Where the leaf comes from.

We nearly called it Linden.

For centuries, villages across Germany and the Netherlands kept a linden tree at their centre. People gathered under it, news was passed on, disputes were heard and judgments were given. They called it the Gerichtslinde, the court linden, and some of them are still standing after five hundred years. There was no archive and no filing system. There was one place everyone knew to go, where what the village knew was shared out loud and stayed alive because people kept using it.

That is close to what we are building, a thousand years early. We did not keep the name, but we kept the leaf.

01 · Where the mark comes from

How we got to this mark.

01

It started as a slash

A forward slash. The character that gives information structure, creates paths, and helps us find things. That felt right for a knowledge company. But / is a character anyone can type, so it was never really ours.

02

Then a linden leaf

While thinking about Ascending’s AI for a human future, we came to the linden tree. The leaf comes from that story.

03

Two leaves, meeting

One leaf is just a logo. Two of them, rotated and overlapping at the waist, show a relationship: your knowledge and the model, meeting in the middle.

04

And the slash came back

At the angle of the original slash, the two leaves read as one stroke. Nothing was thrown away. It all ended up in the same shape.

02 · Colour, and why

Two colours come with a history. One starts here.

Orange

#FF6A00

The Dutch orange. We are built in Amsterdam, and this is the colour the country signs its name with. It is also the colour software uses for something that is live: a running process, an active state, energy.

Accent · mark · rules · one action per view

Navy

#17232B

European institutional blue, pulled down until it is almost ink. It is what infrastructure looks like: sober, permanent, the colour of paperwork that has to hold up. Everything serious we say is set in it.

Ink · dark bands · the default

Pink

#FF2E93

This one is not inherited from anywhere. It is the surprise, and it says we are building something new rather than reissuing enterprise software. It shows up exactly where we want your eye and nowhere else.
